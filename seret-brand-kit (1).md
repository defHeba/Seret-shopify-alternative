# SERET — Brand Kit
*Egyptian perfume brand · extracted from Instagram grid analysis*

## 1. Color Palette

| Swatch | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Parchment Cream | `#F2EDE0` | Primary background — main canvas for ~70% of posts |
| Off-White Cream | `#FFFDF5` | Secondary/lighter background variant |
| Signature Gold-Olive | `#886F24` | Logo wordmark, elegant serif headlines, "premium" accent |
| Ink Black | `#000000` | Bold headline copy, body/comparison text |
| Soft accents (blush pink, kelly green) | varies | Used sparingly in UGC/lifestyle content, not core palette |

**Palette logic:** the brand lives almost entirely in warm neutrals (cream + gold + black). Pink/green show up only in real customer UGC (crafts, kids' content), reinforcing authenticity rather than brand system.

## 2. Typography

**Logo / Wordmark**
- Thin, elegant serif, small size, wide letter-spacing, set in the signature gold-olive
- Always understated — never the largest element on the page (top-left or top-center, small scale)

**Display Serif** (taglines, seasonal drops)
- Elegant, classic serif — mixed case, occasional italic for a single accented word (e.g. *"New"* in "Something *New* is Coming")
- Used for: "Summer 26", "Something New Is Coming," "What if you could go back to this moment..."
- Carries the emotional/aspirational copy

**Bold Display Sans/Gothic** (impact statements)
- Heavy weight, condensed, ALL CAPS, high contrast
- Used for: sale announcements, sports-win hype ("...GYPT WINS 3-...", "30% OFF")
- Reserved for urgency/promo moments layered over photography

**Clean Sans-Serif** (informational/comparison copy)
- Light-medium weight, tracked-out capitals
- Used for: comparison charts ("450 CAL / 0 CAL"), instructional captions ("WHEN YOU NEED A TREAT... CHOOSE WISELY")

**Type hierarchy rule of thumb:** gold serif = brand voice, black bold sans = promo voice, black serif = emotional/story voice.

## 3. Layout & Grid System

- Strict 1:1 Instagram grid tiles, consistent visual rhythm
- Generous negative space — copy sits in the center or lower-third with lots of breathing room
- Logo lockup is small and consistent across almost every post (quiet, not shouty)
- Alternates in a predictable pattern:
  1. **Pure typographic post** (cream bg, serif copy only)
  2. **Bold graphic/photo hybrid** (real photo + heavy sans overlay for promos/news)
  3. **UGC / behind-the-scenes** (real customer photos, handmade crafts, real texture — breaks the polish intentionally)
  4. **Product/customization showcase** (flat-lay style, clean cream background)

## 4. Tone of Voice

- **Culturally plugged-in:** rides real Egyptian moments (football wins) rather than only product pushes — "History was made... we're celebrating it the Seret way."
- **Teaser-driven:** builds anticipation ("Stay Tuned," "Something New Is Coming," "Swipe →")
- **Playful comparison marketing:** self-aware, funny value framing ("0 CAL" perfume vs. a 450-cal treat)
- **Sentimental/nostalgic:** ties scent to memory ("What if you could go back to this moment... with just one spray?")
- **Personalization-forward:** emphasizes customizable cards, labels, and scent as a gifting angle
- Copy is short, punchy, and confident — rarely more than one line of large type per post

## 5. Overall Brand Feel

Warm, quiet luxury with a distinctly Egyptian, community-driven edge. The gold-on-cream system reads premium and minimal, but the brand isn't afraid to get loud (bold black sports hype) or unpolished (real customer UGC) when the moment calls for it — a mix of "affordable indulgence" and "local pride."
